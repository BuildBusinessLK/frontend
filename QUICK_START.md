# 🚀 Quick Start: Download & Deploy to Netlify

## What You Need

- ✅ Your generated website files (HTML, CSS, JS)
- ✅ Netlify account (free)
- ✅ 5 minutes

---

## 3-Step Process

### Step 1️⃣ Generate & Download (In the App)

1. Go to "Create Your Website" in the app
2. Fill in your business details
3. Click "Generate Website"
4. Click **"Download & Deploy"** button
5. Three files download automatically:
   - `index.html`
   - `style.css`
   - `script.js`

### Step 2️⃣ Create Netlify Account (2 minutes)

1. Go to **[app.netlify.com](https://app.netlify.com)**
2. Click **"Sign up"**
3. Choose: GitHub, GitLab, or Email
4. Verify your email

### Step 3️⃣ Deploy Your Site (1 minute)

1. Click **"Add new site"** → **"Deploy manually"**
2. **Drag & drop** your 3 files into the upload area
3. Wait 30-60 seconds
4. **Your site is LIVE!** 🎉

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I edit my site after deploying?**  
A: Yes! Edit the files locally and reupload, or connect Git for auto-updates.

**Q: Can I use my own domain?**  
A: Yes! In Netlify settings → Domain management. (Domains cost extra)

**Q: Will my contact form work?**  
A: Yes! Netlify handles form submissions automatically.

**Q: Is it secure?**  
A: Yes! Free HTTPS/SSL certificate is automatic.

**Q: Can I get more traffic?**  
A: Netlify handles millions of requests. Upgrade to paid for advanced features.
