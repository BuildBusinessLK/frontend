## 🌐 Deploy to Netlify (Easiest - 2 Minutes)

### Quick Steps:

1. **Go to Netlify:**
   - Visit [app.netlify.com](https://app.netlify.com)
   - Sign up (GitHub, GitLab, or Email)

2. **Deploy:**
   - Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
   - Drag & drop your 3 files
   - Wait 30-60 seconds

3. **Done!**
   - Your site is live at: `yoursite.netlify.app`
   - Free HTTPS, global CDN, auto-updates

## 🎓 Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "My website is live!"
→ Congratulations! Share your URL everywhere

### Scenario 2: "I want to edit my site"
→ Edit the files locally and reupload to Netlify

### Scenario 3: "I want a custom domain"
→ Go to Netlify settings → Domain management

### Scenario 4: "Styles aren't loading"
→ Make sure style.css is in same folder as index.html

### Scenario 5: "Form isn't working"
→ Make sure script.js is included in index.html

### Scenario 6: "Site is slow"
→ Netlify should be fast. Clear cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
